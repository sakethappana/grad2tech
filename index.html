Placement commitment

A placement commitment built on shared responsibility.

We commit to placing every eligible candidate. Eligibility is earned through the work you put into the program — and the conditions are set out plainly below.

Demo overview of the proposed placement and refund policy. Final written terms will be provided before enrolment.

What it means

100% placement commitment for eligible candidates.

Candidates who meet every program requirement and attend their placement interviews are covered by the commitment. Grad2Tech prepares them for placement interviews and supports them towards a role. If an eligible candidate remains unplaced, the review process below applies.

To remain eligible, you must
  • Complete the required daily assignments.
  • Complete the required weekly assessments.
  • Meet the program’s participation requirements.
  • Attend the placement interviews arranged for you.

Candidates who do not meet these obligations are not covered by the placement commitment.

If an eligible candidate remains unplaced

The proposed feedback and refund process

  1. Step 1

    Company feedback is requested

    Grad2Tech asks the interviewing companies for feedback on the candidate’s interviews.

  2. Step 2

    The reasons are reviewed together

    Grad2Tech and the candidate review the reasons given across those interviews.

  3. Step 3

    Fees are refunded on mutual agreement

    Under the proposed policy, fees are refunded when Grad2Tech and the candidate mutually agree that the reasons provided are genuine.

An unsuccessful interview does not automatically trigger a refund. The process applies only to eligible candidates who remain unplaced after taking part in their interviews.
